## Problem Statement

You are given an array of daily stock prices. Choose when to buy and sell to earn the maximum profit. You may complete multiple transactions, but can hold at most one share at a time. You may sell and buy again on the same day. Try this independently before reading a solution.


## Input and Output

Input  An integer array prices, where prices[i] is the stock price on day i.

Output  The maximum profit achievable by trading under the one-share rule.


## Examples

| # | Input | Output | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | prices = [7,1,5,3,6,4] | 7 | The maximum profit for this price series is 7. |
| 2 | prices = [1,2,3,4,5] | 4 | The maximum profit for this price series is 4. |
| 3 | prices = [7,6,4,3,1] | 0 | No transaction can produce a profit. |

## Constraints

1 <= prices.length <= 3 * 10^4 • 0 <= prices[i] <= 10^4
