## Problem Statement

Given an uppercase string s and an integer k, you may change at most k characters. Return the greatest length of a contiguous substring that can be made entirely one repeated letter. Try the problem before reading a solution or hint.


## Input and Output

Input  An uppercase English string s and an integer k.

Output  The maximum length of a substring that can be made of one repeated letter.


## Examples

| # | Input | Output | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | s = "ABAB", k = 2 | 4 | With two changes, the whole substring can be made one repeated letter. |
| 2 | s = "AABABBA", k = 1 | 4 | One change can create a four-character substring of the same letter. |

## Constraints

1 <= s.length <= 10^5 • s consists only of uppercase English letters • 0 <= k <= s.length
